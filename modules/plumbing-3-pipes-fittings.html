Quick guide

  • Supply = clean water under pressure. Blue = cold, red = hot. Pipes: PEX, copper, CPVC. Old homes: galvanized steel.
  • Drain (DWV) = drain, waste, vent. No pressure, just gravity. Pipes: PVC, ABS. Old homes: cast iron.
  • PEX color is just a label. Red, blue, and white are the same pipe.
  • Never use PVC or ABS for hot water supply.
  • Copper meeting galvanized steel? Put a dielectric union between them.
